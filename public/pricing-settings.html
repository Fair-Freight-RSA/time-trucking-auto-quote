Commercial Pricing Rules

Time Trucking commercial rules can change the customer selling price after the applicable Time Trucking base rate has been selected.

Day vs Per-km Pricing Rule Pending Henning confirmation

Day vs km pricing rule has not yet been confirmed. Automatic quotes requiring this decision must remain review-required.

Additional collection/delivery stop Confirmed

The initial collection and final delivery are not additional stops. R1,500 applies to each additional collection/delivery stop.

Driver night-out allowance Rate confirmed / automatic trigger pending

The R1,750 allowance is added when a driver is required to sleep out. The automatic rule for determining when a night out is required has not yet been confirmed.

10% Minimum Protection Rule Pending Henning confirmation

Normal profit is already included in the Time Trucking commercial rate card. The 10% rule will not be applied as an additional markup until its exact meaning is confirmed.

VAT

VAT is applied to the commercial subtotal. If this remains 0%, VAT configuration requires confirmation before automatic customer quoting.

Raw litres x diesel price remains internal cost analysis only. Current diesel below baseline does not reduce Henning's commercial rate unless an approved rule says to.

CraneManual crane cost required

No generic crane price is used. Required crane work must have a supplier/reference and amount.

ForkliftOperational info only

No automatic forklift charge. Use third-party handling cost only if Time Trucking arranges it.

High-value cargoInsurance review required

No generic insurance percentage is used. Additional insurance requires approved policy/rate or manual insurer cost.

Additional Hazardous-Goods External Cost

HAZ cargo selects the HAZ commercial rate. External statutory/permit/escort costs remain separate and quote-specific.
